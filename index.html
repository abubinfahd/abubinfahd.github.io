About Me

🎓 M.Sc. Statistics — Khulna University 💼 AI/ML Engineer · Data Scientist 🏅 NST Fellow — Gov. of Bangladesh 📖 Springer Author · 2 Book Chapters

I am a Jr. Data Scientist at ADN Diginet Ltd in Dhaka, Bangladesh, specializing in the intersection of statistical modeling and machine learning engineering. I bring hands-on experience deploying production-grade systems across the full model lifecycle—from fine-tuning and evaluation to real-time inference and scalable deployment. Previously, I worked as an AI/ML Engineer at Technonext Software Limited, where I built and launched core systems in bilingual NLP, computer vision, and recommendation architectures.

My engineering work focuses on high-impact production solutions: bilingual (Bangla/English) NLP parsing, large language model (LLM) workflows, and real-time computer vision applications (including anti-spoofing liveness verification, gaze tracking, and attention monitoring). These are typically served as high-performance microservices using PyTorch, Transformers, Kafka, and vector search (FAISS, ChromaDB).

Academically, I hold an M.Sc. in Statistics (Thesis Track) from Khulna University with a CGPA of 3.79 / 4.00 (Distinction). Recognized with the prestigious NST Fellowship by the Government of Bangladesh for research excellence, I co-authored two peer-reviewed Springer book chapters focusing on dimensionality reduction and medical AI applications.

Dedicated to education and community growth, I also serve as an ML & Data Analytics Mentor at Shohoj Coding, delivering structured curricula that translate statistical theory into industry-ready data science skills.
