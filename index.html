About Me

I'm an AI/ML Engineer at Technonext Software Limited in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with production experience across the full ML lifecycle — data engineering, model fine-tuning, evaluation, and deployment.

My work spans bilingual Bangla/English NLP, LLM-based generative workflows, recommendation infrastructure, and computer vision systems — deployed via FastAPI and gRPC microservices using PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, Kafka, and vector retrieval (FAISS, ChromaDB).

My academic foundation is an M.Sc. in Statistics (CGPA 3.80/4.00) from Khulna University, with a thesis-track focused on deep learning for OCR. I hold an NST Fellowship from the Government of Bangladesh for academic and research excellence.

I'm actively pursuing PhD opportunities in AI/ML, with interests in LLMs, Agentic AI, Clinical AI, Causal ML, and Trustworthy AI.
